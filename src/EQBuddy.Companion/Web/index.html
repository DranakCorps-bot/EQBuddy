This page needs a pairing code to show anything.

On your PC, open EQBuddy → Options → Behavior → EQBuddy Mobile and scan the QR code there — it carries the code for you.

Nothing is shown, stored, or sent without it.

Screens on this device

Saved on this phone or tablet only — every device picks its own. On a narrow screen the picks stack down the page; on a tablet they lay out side by side in this order, with the map taking the big panel. The PC decides which screens are offered at all (EQBuddy → Options).
No browser chrome: tap ⛶ at the top for this visit, or use your browser's Add to Home Screen — that launches EQBuddy Mobile in its own window with no address bar at all, and it survives closing the tab.
