## Acceptance (the card's, restated as the D2 review checklist)

A never-used EQ Legends player, in 30–45 seconds of scanning: knows what EQBuddy is (hero),
what problems it solves (section 2), why Evolved beats a DPS/log parser (sections 2+4 — it
remembers and connects), what it looks like (every section shows, not tells), that it never
reads memory or judges others (quiet line + section 5), and where to download (two CTAs).
Nobody is forced through architecture, history, or methodology to get there — all three still
exist, one quiet link away.

## What this plan deliberately does not do

No app code, no new product claims beyond §1's audit, no brand/palette/typography change, no
new site pages (the deep dive's home is repo docs, keeping the tested surface at one HTML
file), no removal of the Support link or footer disclaimers, no release action of any kind —
the v2 release go stays David's, untouched; this page merely stops contradicting it when it
happens.
