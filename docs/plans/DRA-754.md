## 2. What the player gets

A ninth goal that answers. Pick **Achievements** and the Helper ranks the **unvisited
world places** from your own dump, keyed on the ZONE like every other engine. A place
that also serves LevelUp or Farm Gear merges into that zone's row (the existing
`Join`). Each row says, with no adjectives (HOME-006):

- *Personal* (the dump): which Explorer it advances and where that stands ("Faydwer
  Explorer: 13 of 15 visited").
- *Catalog*: the route from the current zone (`TravelPlan` hops + path, the Routes tab's
  own module), and the eqlwiki band where one exists ("creatures here: 30–60+, eqlwiki").
- Not ranked, but said once under the rows: the unread places by name (cap 3 + "and N
  more", trap 50) and one line counting the 16 housing/guild places EQBuddy has no map
  for. The subject of both sentences is EQBuddy's data, never the game.

No dump means the existing `NoAchievementsDump` gap, with the
`GameCommands.OutputfileAchievements` copy button (the command ships, per the standing
rule).

**Out of scope:** Hunter, Slayer, every other section, kill counters, any tick or write
to the dump's state, era gating (`ZoneEras` has no reader and WorldEra starts ABSENT, so
an era rule here would be invented), and any alias table.

## 3. Ruling for Helm at SIGN

**R1 — what the level does to an exploration target.** A visit is not a camp. Zoning in
and out completes it, but the zone's creatures are still the risk.
- **(a)** Refuse, bottom arm only: reuse `BandGate`'s `GearBandReachAbove` (5) and refuse
  a place whose band bottom is 5+ over the character. Report the refusals with their own
  count. The top ("outgrown") arm stands down, because a low zone is still a valid visit.
- **(b) — recommended.** Keep every place, rank in-band places first, and put the band
  sentence on the row. Unknown band or unknown level changes nothing (trap 73).

Recommendation: **(b)**. A player chasing "Faydwer Explorer" wants the whole list. A
refusal would hide the place they are asking about, and the band sentence states the
risk without calling anything safe. Either way `LevelUseFor(Achievements)` becomes
`Consumes`, and the must-list test proves it at two levels. Unsigned → (b).

Everything else is decided here and logged at D1 in `DECISIONS.md`, per the reporting
duty.
