## §3 Where the gate attaches to the Soft loop — and the one place it must not

### §3.1 The attach point

EQBuddy's Soft loop has exactly one plan-SIGN ask: **a LIVE ASK appended to
`HELM-FEEDBACK.md`, pushed, followed by the `helm-back-channel.yml` wake.**
That is the ask the merged SPEC §3's scope line names (*"the gate fires on
plan-SIGN asks"*), and it is the only place the gate attaches.

So: **a plan that trips C1–C5 carries its `challenge:` line before its LIVE ASK
goes up.** The line is written into the plan body (`docs/plans/DRA-<n>.md`,
beside the `route:` tag) and restated in the ask. Verdict semantics, the
gate-outcome mapping (the four SIGNed verdicts; `HOLD` only ever as
gate-outcome shape, never a verdict), the `challenge-overrule:` line and the
6-hour fail-open NO-RETURN rule are **the merged SPEC §4's and are inherited
verbatim** — this card does not re-specify them and must not drift from them.

Two of §4's rules deserve restating here because they bind the **waker**, and
in this loop the waker is this repo's Planner:

- **Silence after a wake is never a pass.** "No test fires → silence" and
  "waked → no line" are different events, and no EQBuddy doc may merge them.
  Once a wake has fired, an absent line is a **non-return**, disposed by the
  6-hour NO-RETURN line — never read as PROCEED.
- **The plan slot is capped, and the check is the waker's** (merged §5 —
  the charter's own guard is downstream of the failure, which DRA-306's
  truncated run proved in production). Before waking, **Planner measures the
  plan artifact in bytes and records it in the wake**. For an EQBuddy plan
  the artifact is the committed `docs/plans/DRA-<n>.md`. Ceiling **17,343 B**
  (derived from the worst observed floor, not chosen). Over the ceiling →
  **challenged by named sections, never truncated**: Planner names the spans
  carrying the load-bearing premises. A plan that cannot be reduced under the
  ceiling is a charter §6.6 plane-question reopen and goes to Helm as such,
  carrying the NO-RETURN line — never waked-and-hoped. This file is itself
  over that ceiling and takes the named-sections branch for its own walk
  (§7.2), exactly as the merged SPEC did for itself.

### §3.2 The seam that matters more than the attach point

**The gate fires ONCE, at the plan's SIGN. It never fires per slice.**

This is the single most important EQBuddy-side statement in this file, and it is
not a convenience. `docs/ops/execution-flow.md` §4 is the measurement: a signed
plan authorizes every slice it declares, in order, on green gates, because in the
DRA-70/71/72 window three Helm coverage gaps produced stalls of 3.5–6.4 h
accounting for **15.3 of 18.2 total wait-hours — 84%** — all of it planned work
parked at an authorization boundary, while **Helm's pre-merge change rate over
the same window was ~0%**.

A per-slice Challenger wake would re-create, one layer down, precisely the cost
that cutover bought out. The gate is allowed to add writing and one bounded
wait to a *plan*; it is not allowed to add a boundary to a *slice sequence*.

**The seam is the one that already exists.** A slice that exceeds the plan's
declared files, behaviour or boundary stops and escalates — and that escalation
*is* a new plan-SIGN ask, so it takes the gate at §3.1 like any other. Nothing
new is invented to catch it.

### §3.3 What does not trip the gate, stated so the default stays off

- **V0–V1 work.** It never reaches a plan-SIGN ask. The routing table in
  `CLAUDE.md` is untouched.
- **Every slice after D1 of a signed plan.** §3.2.
- **The inboxes.** `SCRIBE.md`, `BEVEL.md`, `FABLE.md` inform; they never
  trigger an unattended agent, and they do not wake the Challenger either.
- **Helm's operational doors** — a release click, a posture signature on a
  public reply, a hold. The merged SPEC §3 rejected these by name, on record: the charter scopes
  the role to *"the reasoning behind consequenceful research, strategy and
  plans,"* and a release click has no plan-reasoning to challenge. Where a
  door-touching *plan* exists, C1–C5 catch it upstream.
- **The consequence list.** Those eight are David's, and they are decided with
  the question tool. A Challenger argument does not insert itself in front of
  the Founder. Where a consequence-list item also has a plan, the plan takes the
  gate; the door still goes to David, unchanged.
- **Reviewer diffs, Sr quick-passes, rotation PRs carrying out a SIGNed rule.**
  A DRA-154 rotation executes a standing rule; it neither re-opens nor changes
  one.

## §4 C1–C5, rendered against this repo's own history

**C1 — reverses, narrows, or re-opens a prior SIGN or ruling. Absolute.**
*Fires:* DRA-267's narrowing of the "Executor never trims" bar; DRA-287 moving
`FABLE.md` between `channel-size-guard` arms; any ask to raise or add a row in

*Fires:* DRA-179's Jr/Sr router; DRA-267's widening of the rotation seat to any
Soft seat; DRA-107's claim-seat call-site rule. *Does not fire:* claiming a seat.

*Fires:* DRA-292's `max_tokens: 8192` on the shared `hermes_local` config — a
