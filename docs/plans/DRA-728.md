## 1. Verdict

**The direction is sound. The plan is too big, and three of its premises are wrong.**

The brief gets the important thing right. EQBuddy's Helper is already the stronger
recommender, and the work is about what evidence goes INTO it, not replacing it. Its
non-goals (§13) are also right.

What the code says it gets wrong:

1. **The source.** It proposes harvesting Alanna's Race Unlock Guide. eqlwiki's own
   *All Positive Faction Quests* table and the `facblock`s on individual quest pages are
   already in the **committed** cache (`scripts/harvests/eqlwiki/cache/`). They hold the turn-in
   item, the per-turn-in amount for each faction, the entry requirement and how the item is
   obtained. Using them fetches nothing, so no harvest un-PARK is needed. They are the wiki's
   structured data rather than one author's recommended method, and they follow the
   `ZoneLevels`/`ZoneEras`/`ZoneMerchants` precedent exactly.
2. **What "readiness" can be measured from.** The brief assumes Plane of Sky pieces are read
   from the bags. They are not. `UnlockGuidance.Sky` counts `SkyQuestChecklistItem.Acquired`
   (`UnlockGuidance.cs:370`). That is a checklist tick set by loot auto-tick, a manual tick or
   the achievements import, and nothing ever UN-ticks it when an item leaves the bags. "All
   pieces are in your bags" is therefore not a claim the code can make today.
3. **Where leverage comes from.** Phase 5's flagship example (Blackburrow → Gnoll Fangs →
   Rogues of the White Rose → Barbarian) is **not what eqlwiki says**. The wiki's Barbarian
   route is the Lion Meat Shipment quest: Lion Delight handed in to Halas factions
   (`quest-Lion_Meat_Shipment_Quest.wikitext`, where Rogues of the White Rose moves +5). More
   generally, most race-unlock routes are vendor-bought or crafted items handed in inside a
   CITY: wine, muffins, brandy, Tumpy Tonics. They are not drops at a camp. The Helper joins on
   ZONE, so the "one zone serves Level Up + Unlock" result the brief builds Phase 5 on can only
   occur for the minority of routes that are kill-drops: by Alanna's page, Froglok's ghouls,
   Erudite's kobolds, Halfling's goblins and Troll's Deathfist belts. That count is from a
   reading of the page today and has not been measured against the cache. An invented example
   in a plan becomes a fixture, and a fixture becomes a claim (trap 73).

## 3. What to reject or simplify

- **REJECT the six-value evidence taxonomy (§3.4, §11).** "GameOutput" is already Personal: its
  doc covers "this player's own log, dumps or bags". A route from an eqlwiki page is Catalog,
  the same as every other eqlwiki number. "Derived" is a property of a SENTENCE, not a source,
  and the rule is that arithmetic takes the evidence class of its weakest input. Six values
  would be six things every surface has to style, and none of them changes a decision. The one
  thing the brief really needs is for a catalog line to **name its page**, which a
  `WordedFact` string already can.
- **REJECT the seven-state readiness enum (§5.3).** `NeedsTravel`, `Farmable` and `KnownPath`
  are not distinctions EQBuddy can measure, and a state nothing can measure is a label waiting
  to be filled with a guess (trap 73). Readiness is ONE fact with two values: a criterion is
  **ready now** (provable from a dump), or it is not. Everything else is already said by the
  existing lines.
- **REJECT Phase 5 as a phase.** Leverage is `Goals.Count`, which already sorts first. More
  legitimate joins come from engines emitting zones, not from a new concept.
- **DEFER the generalized dependency graph (§6–7, §10.3). It is not justified now.** The two
  domain joins it would replace (`QuestCatalog.ByItem` and `GuideAttachment`) are fine. The
  rule is the brief's own: extract a graph on the THIRD repeated structure, not the first. The
  faction-route reader in §6 would be the second. Re-assess after S3 lands.
- **DEFER the Achievements goal (Phase 3) behind a survey.** The dump does carry counters:
  `Slayer` rows read `162/5000` over a creature FAMILY ("Beetles, Cliknars, … Spiders"),
  measured in `tests/fixtures/achievements/hateborne.txt`. But nothing in EQBuddy maps a
  creature family to a zone, so the brief's "8 frogs remain in Upper Guk" has no producer.
  Building one would be a new curated catalog. Most counters are also thousands away, so
  "what to do next" is not a sentence they support honestly. Survey first (S4).
- **DO NOT harvest Alanna's guide in this program.** It is not in the committed cache, so
  using it needs a fetch, which is a Helm harvest ruling. Its "Recommended Method" is one
  player's opinion, where the faction table is structured wiki data. It can be a later
  addition, for its *ordering* advice only, once S1 measures what the table does not cover.

## 4. Data findings (measured 2026-10-01, committed cache)

- 930 cached quest pages; **503** carry a `facblock`; **150** carry a numeric amount
  ("has been adjusted by N"). The rest say only "got better/worse", which gives a direction
  with no number. That makes it route-only, with no arithmetic (the brief's §4.5 rule).
- `quest-All_Positive_Faction_Quests.wikitext` is about 15 table rows: item × count →
  faction (+N), requirement ("Apprehensive Dark Bargainers"), and how the item is obtained
  ("Purchased Item"). Some rows say `(+?)`, and those are route-only.
- `guides-transform.py:121–128` deliberately DROPS facblocks today, and `quests-harvest.py`
  does not extract them. The data is in the cache and absent from everything shipped.
- No faction TIER table exists in code ("Apprehensive" and "Amiable" appear nowhere in
  `src/`), so a route's entry requirement can only be **shown verbatim**, never checked.
  Checking it would need a curated tier table. That is out of scope here and named as a gap.

## 5. Ranking: the trap in the brief's acceptance criterion

Phase 2's AC1 asks that "a fully assembled PoSky turn-in can rank ahead of a
numerically-more-complete unlock". **That cannot be done inside the unlock engine.** The
engine's own sort only picks which six candidates survive (`Recommendations.cs:3761–3766`).
The final order is then `Rank()`'s: `Goals.Count → HasPersonalEvidence → Weight → Subject`.
Every unlock row is `HasPersonalEvidence` (its `UnlockScoreFact` is Personal), so the order
between unlocks falls through to `Weight`, which is the completion fraction.

So there are exactly two honest options:

- **(a)** Add `ReadyNow` as a `Rank()` key between `Goals.Count` and `HasPersonalEvidence`.
  This is ordinal, with no invented weight, and only unlock and faction-route rows ever set it.
  But it is a **cross-engine** change: a ready turn-in outranks a one-goal personal XP camp.
- **(b)** Use readiness only inside the unlock engine's pick and tie-break, and accept that
  ready unlocks do not jump other engines' rows.

Planner recommends (a). It changes what the Helper puts first, though, so it is Founder
question 2.

Separately, there is a pre-existing divergence S3 must close (trap 4): the Unlocks TAB ticks
faction rows from the faction dump (`UnlockLayout.cs:106–117`), while the Helper ranks on
`UnlockProgress.Score`, which reads the achievements dump's C/I flags. With a fresh faction
dump and an old achievements dump, the two disagree about how far along an unlock is.

## 6. Proposed slices (NOT authorized; filed only after §7 is answered)

**S1 — `faction-routes-transform.py` → `Core/Data/FactionRoutes.json` + `Core/FactionRoutes.cs`.**
Reads the committed cache only and fetches nothing. Plain JSON, with `--check` and
`--selftest` in `check.ps1` and CI (the `ZoneEras` pattern; trap 74 cannot arise).
- **Admit:** a turn-in item, a count per turn-in, and a faction with an integer delta. Taken
  from the All-Positive table and from quest-page facblocks, joined to the quest's turn-in
  items through `QuestCatalog`.
- **Carry:** the requirement text and the obtain text VERBATIM, plus the source page title.
- **Refuse BY NAME, and report:** `(+?)`, direction-only blocks, and a quest whose items cannot
  be joined.
- **Keep costs:** negative deltas are carried, because a route that tanks an opposing faction
  is the first thing a grinder needs to know.
- **Guard:** distinct-count telltale on the amounts, a must-list naming every race-unlock
  faction, and each one's status (routed / direction-only / none). A survey floor: if fewer
  than half the race-unlock factions get a numeric route, STOP and escalate. That would be
  the point where Alanna's page earns a Helm harvest ask.
- No reader is added in this slice.

**S2 — Cold-start arm in `UnlockGuidance.Faction`.**
- **When it fires:** only when the pool has NO raiser for the faction. Personal evidence keeps
  the row exactly as it is today, plus at most one "the wiki also lists …" line.
- **What it emits:** route lines as `Evidence.Catalog`, each naming its eqlwiki page. Turn-ins
  to go is `ceil(PointsToMax / delta)` **per faction**, said per faction. A multi-faction
  route's real number is the max over its factions, and a single number would hide which
  faction is the bottleneck.
- **What the player holds:** read from the inventory dump through `InventoryFile` (new
  `HelperInputs.Bags` init property). An absent dump is the existing `NoInventoryDump` sentence
  and is never "0 held".
- **Door:** the existing `GeneralTabQuest` door when the quest is in the catalog. No new door
  kind.
- **Zone:** the quest's catalog zone where there is one, so a kill-drop route can join a
  camp. A city turn-in joins nothing, honestly.
- **Phone:** parity row on the phone page, including the page-side must-list.

**S3 — Readiness, as one fact.**
- **The fact:** `ReadyNow` on a criterion is true only when a DUMP proves it.
  - A Sky reward is ready when the inventory dump holds every piece of it and it is not
    marked turned in.
  - A faction route is ready when the held turn-in items cover at least one turn-in.
  - A checklist tick alone is NOT ready, and the line says "the Sky checklist says all pieces
    acquired". That is the honest version of what the code knows today.
- **Ranking:** a `Rank()` key per Founder answer 2.
- **Close the divergence:** the Score / faction-dump divergence in §5 is closed in this slice.
- **Tests:** the brief's §9 Phase 2 list, plus "a checklist tick with an inventory dump that
  lacks the item is NOT ready" as the committed negative.

**S4 — Achievements survey (Researcher; read-only; no product code).**
Over the two committed fixtures: which sections have counters, and how many rows could name a
PLACE from data EQBuddy already ships. Output is a go / no-go for an Achievements engine and,
if go, its first family. Expected: Hunter or Exploration rather than Slayer. That is a
hypothesis.

Order: S1 → S2 → S3. S4 can run any time.
