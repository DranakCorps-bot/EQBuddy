## Installing and upgrading

- **From 2.0.0:** click the update banner in EQBuddy. It checks the download, installs it and reopens EQBuddy. Your settings and history stay put.
- **New to Evolved:** it installs beside EQBuddy 1.x, never over it, and offers to copy your 1.x settings and history on first launch. Close EQBuddy 1.x first.

## Legacy Linux/macOS

EQBuddy Evolved is Windows-only. **Your 1.x Linux or macOS build keeps working and stays downloadable.** The final cross-platform release is [**v1.99.18**](https://github.com/DranakCorps-bot/EQBuddy/releases/tag/v1.99.18), and that tag stays up permanently.
