## Installing and upgrading

- **Installs beside EQBuddy 1.x, never over it.** It has its own program folder, Start-menu entry and settings.
- **Bring your history with you.** On first launch Evolved offers to copy your 1.x settings and history. Close EQBuddy 1.x first.
- **Updates itself** from its own releases from here on.
- **Privacy:** Evolved asks once whether it may send an anonymous "still running" heartbeat. If you say yes, it sends a random ID, the app version and your Windows version, nothing about your character or play, and no IP address is stored. **No** is a complete answer. You can change it any time in Options → Behavior. [What exactly is sent →](https://github.com/DranakCorps-bot/EQBuddy/blob/main/docs/Telemetry.md)

## Fixes

- The minimised bar no longer flicks through old sessions' numbers while EQBuddy starts.
- The Plane of Sky tab no longer ticks items you don't have, and folded boxes stay folded. *(Hateborne, #691)*
- A hall wizard in Lower Guk no longer starts the arch magi respawn chip. *(bjordan2010, #394)*
- EQBuddy could freeze every number while still looking fine. Fixed.
- Shield of Thorns is named and timed properly again.
- Your settings survive an update, even if EQBuddy is closed mid-save.
- A new phone could get stuck on "connecting…" after scanning the pairing code. Fixed.

## Legacy Linux/macOS

EQBuddy Evolved is Windows-only. **Your 1.x Linux or macOS build keeps working and stays downloadable.** The final cross-platform release is [**v1.99.18**](https://github.com/DranakCorps-bot/EQBuddy/releases/tag/v1.99.18). It has `EQBuddy-linux-x64.tar.gz`, `EQBuddy-osx-arm64.zip` and `EQBuddy-osx-x64.zip`, and that tag stays up permanently. Nothing on your machine is changed or removed. Thank you to **Don Thompson**, who built and carried the cross-platform app, and **quasarj**, for macOS click-through, spoken alerts and Wine log detection.
