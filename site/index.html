What can EQBuddy help me do?

Every capture and clip on this page is a real build driven by the repo’s own harness — character names are test fixtures.

“What should I do in Plane of Sky?”

The Guide pins the NEXT step and names who, where and what. Loot in your log ticks the checklist by itself.

“How do I upgrade this?”

It starts from what you are wearing and names the creature and the zone that drop a better one.

“Where should I hunt?”

Every camp you sit is measured — kills, drops, rates. The Helper ranks zones from that evidence, and marks one down once you have outgrown it.

“When will it spawn?”

Spawn timers learned from your own kills, counting down beside the map.

Built for playing, not managing software

While you play, EQBuddy is one thin tray of the numbers you chose. Everything deeper is a gesture away, and nothing interrupts.

  • Star what you watch — the tray builds itself from it.
  • Hover to peek, click to keep — the card stays open and live.
  • Drag to reorder — and park the card wherever you want it.

Your character. Your history. Your guide.

Not a generic “best in slot” list. EQBuddy learns from your own play and connects it — gear → quest → mob → camp → route.

Gear

Which upgrades beat what you are wearing now.

Quest

Which quest provides it, and what you can already turn in.

Mob

Which creatures drop it, and whether the zone fits your level.

Camp

Spawn timers that learn from your kills.

Route

The zone map, with the camp and where you stand from your own /loc.

You never re-enter a fact EQBuddy already read from your log.

On your desktop, and on the phone you look away to — LAN-only, optional, no account.

Built differently

Private by design

Local-first. No account, no cloud; telemetry is opt-in only.

Safe for EQ

It reads what the game writes for you — the /log it tails live and the /outputfile dumps you ask for: inventory, achievements, faction, spellbook. No game-memory reads, no automation, no injection.

Personal, not competitive

It measures only you — never a way to judge other players.

Evidence over guesses

Observed and estimated are labelled apart. A precise-looking wrong number is refused.
